## What is ron.cool?

ron.cool is basically my **personal corner of the internet**.

It's where I build whatever I feel like building.

Sometimes that's a game.

Sometimes it's a stupid little experiment.

Sometimes it's a completely unnecessary website that somehow takes 3 days to make.

And sometimes it turns into something way bigger.

> **If it's interesting, weird, fun or worth making — it belongs on ron.cool.**
