SECURITY

Keeping Ron.cool safe.

Found a security vulnerability on Ron.cool? Report it privately and help us keep the site safe for everyone.

🛡️ Responsible disclosure

Please report security issues privately instead of publicly sharing them. This gives us time to investigate and fix problems.

Please don't exploit real users.

Don't access, modify, delete or download information belonging to other users. Only test information you own or have permission to test.

🔎 What should I report?

Examples of security issues we'd like to hear about:

1
Account & authentication Issues that could allow unauthorized account access.
2
Data exposure Unexpected access to private or sensitive information.
3
Website vulnerabilities XSS, injection, access-control issues or similar problems.
4
Security bypasses Ways to bypass protections or restrictions unexpectedly.

📋 Make a good report

The more information you provide, the easier it is for us to investigate.

1
Explain it Tell us what you discovered and why it may be a security problem.
2
Reproduce it Give us clear steps that allow us to reproduce the issue.
3
Show evidence Screenshots, console output, requests and proof-of-concepts can be useful.

⏱️ What happens next?

We'll review the report, investigate the issue and attempt to reproduce it.

We take legitimate reports seriously.

If the vulnerability is confirmed, we'll work on fixing it and update its status internally.
