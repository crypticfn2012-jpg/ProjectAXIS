How to Play

🪐 Stay in Orbit

Your little ship automatically orbits the central planet.

🎯 Collect Stars

Move your cursor around the screen. Collect glowing stars to increase your score.

☄️ Avoid Meteors

Meteors will appear as your score increases. Touching one ends the run.

🔥 Survive

The longer you survive, the faster everything becomes.

Instructions

🖱 Mouse

Move your mouse around the game area to control your ship.

📱 Mobile

Drag your finger around the screen to move.

⏸ Pause

Press P or click the pause button in the top-right corner.

🏆 Best Score

Your best score is automatically saved to your browser.
